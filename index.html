00 — The problem

Complaint portals collect problems. They don't solve them.

Citizens already report broken streetlights, flooded drains, and unsafe crossings — through apps, helplines, and ward offices. The report gets logged. It rarely gets engineered.

Meanwhile, thousands of engineering and design students need real problems to work on, and thousands of MSMEs have prototyping capacity sitting idle between contracts. CivicConnect connects the three: the person who has the problem, the lab that can study it, and the industry that can build it — with government watching the whole handoff instead of only the first step.

02 — Who's on the platform

Four roles, one shared pipeline.

Everyone sees the same report move through the same stages — just from their own side of the desk.

Citizens

Report what they see

Submit a problem in under a minute and follow it through triage, matching, and resolution — no repeat visits to a ward office.

Government

Monitor without chasing

See every report's status — matched, in progress, prototyped, deployed — across departments, in one dashboard.

Universities & students

Work on real problems

Turn local civic issues into capstone projects and research, with a verified problem owner and a path to deployment.

Industry & MSMEs

Mentor and prototype

Support student-built solutions with materials, fabrication, and manufacturing know-how, ahead of full deployment.
