Why this exists

Right now, a good idea has nowhere to go.

Citizens notice problems every day — a broken streetlight, an unsafe water source, a failing local system. Universities have people who could solve them. Industry has the money and manufacturing to build the fix. Today, none of them are in the same room.

First

Reports get scattered

A challenge reported by a citizen today lives in a WhatsApp group, a phone call, or a form that nobody downstream ever sees again.

Second

Nobody is introduced

Universities, industry, and government each work in isolation, so the people who could solve a problem never meet the people who reported it.

Third

Good work goes untracked

Even when a solution does get built, there's no shared record of what happened — so it can't be trusted, funded again, or repeated elsewhere.

How it works

From a photo on a phone to a working project.

Every step is visible to everyone involved — the citizen who reported it can watch their problem move all the way to a finished project.

01

A citizen submits the problem

Text, a photo, and a location pin — that's all it takes. No form fields to hunt for, no account to explain the issue twice.

02

The platform reads and sorts it

Gemini-powered analysis categorizes the issue, judges urgency, pulls out keywords, writes a short summary, and checks whether it's a duplicate of something already reported.

AI-assisted, not AI-decided
03

The right university gets matched

The system recommends departments and faculty whose expertise fits the problem, and a student-faculty team can pick it up and validate it.

04

A solution gets proposed

The team drafts an approach. Industry and MSME partners can step in with mentoring, CSR funding, or manufacturing support.

05

It gets built and deployed

Prototyping moves to real deployment, with progress visible to everyone who's been following the problem since day one.

06

Government sees the whole picture

A live dashboard tracks every open issue and every project in motion — so what works in one district can be scaled to the next.

The four portals

Everyone gets a view built for their job.

The same problem looks different depending on who's looking at it. NexGenX shows each participant only what they need.

For the person who noticed the problem

Report it once, then watch it move.

Submit an issue with a photo and a location, and follow its progress from first read to finished project — no chasing anyone for updates.

  • Submit with text, photo, and map location
  • Track status from submitted to resolved
  • See which university or team picked it up
For faculty and student teams

Find the problems that fit your expertise.

Get matched to reports in your department's field, form a team, and submit a solution without ever leaving the platform.

  • Problems pre-matched to relevant departments
  • Assign mentors and build student teams
  • Submit and revise solution proposals
For companies and MSMEs

Back the ideas worth building.

Step into a project at whatever stage suits you — funding a prototype, mentoring a student team, or supplying manufacturing capacity.

  • Offer mentoring, CSR funding, or prototyping support
  • See which projects need what kind of help
  • Track a project through to implementation
For policymakers and administrators

See every issue, everywhere, at once.

Monitor open challenges and active projects across regions, and spot which local fixes are ready to become policy.

  • Live map of issues by category and region
  • Track resolution progress across districts
  • Identify solutions ready to scale
For anyone measuring impact

Turn activity into evidence.

Cross-cutting insight into where problems cluster, how fast they're resolved, and which partnerships are producing results.

  • Break down issues by domain and location
  • Compare priority levels against resolution speed
  • Report on project-status trends over time
